# Memory

The difference between an assistant that's useful in month three and one that
still asks you the same questions.

## What it is

Claude saves notes about your business as you work — small files, one fact each.
Next time you open that folder, they load automatically.

Three months in, a good memory means Claude knows your sites, your systems, which
numbers lie to you, and what you've already ruled out. You stop re-explaining.

## It is per folder

Memory is filed against the folder you had connected. Open a different folder and
none of it loads. This is the single most common surprise — see
`folders-and-memory.md`.

## What belongs in it

The things that aren't written down anywhere else and that would make Claude
confidently wrong without them:

- **How you define things.** Your definition of "active member" or "a sale" is
  probably not the software's definition. Yours is the one that matters.
- **The traps.** Numbers that look right and aren't. Reports that run late so
  recent months always read low.
- **Decisions and why.** Not just what you chose — what you rejected and the reason.
  This stops the same debate happening in six weeks.
- **Corrections.** When Claude gets something wrong and you fix it, that fix should
  survive the conversation.

## What does not belong in it

- Anything the files or the software already say — that's just stale duplication
- Passwords, keys, account numbers
- Anything that only mattered for today's conversation

## How to actually build one

**Say it out loud when you correct something.** The useful sentence is:

> "Remember that — a sale only counts when the money actually clears, not when
> the booking is made."

**At the end of a decent session, ask:**

> "What's worth remembering from this? Anything I corrected you on, anything we
> decided, anything that would save us time next week."

Then check what it writes. If it's a restatement of something obvious, tell it to
drop it. A memory full of filler is worse than an empty one.

**Review it occasionally.** Ask: *"Show me what you remember about this business."*
Delete anything that's gone out of date. Memories are a snapshot of when they were
written, not live truth.

## The relationship with house rules

Two different things, often confused:

| | Where | What it's for |
|---|---|---|
| **House rules** | `CLAUDE.md` in the folder | Stable. The business, the systems, the rules. You write it. |
| **Memory** | Saved automatically | Grows. Facts, corrections, decisions. Claude writes it. |

When a memory has proved itself over weeks, promote it into the house rules file.
That's how the rules file gets good without you having to sit down and write it.
